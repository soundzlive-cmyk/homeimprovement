Additions

More room without moving: bedrooms, family rooms and bump-outs built to match your home.

Love your neighborhood but need more space? An addition gives you the room you need, whether that's a new bedroom, a bigger kitchen, a family room or a mudroom, without the cost and upheaval of moving.

We build additions start to finish with our own crew: planning and permits, foundation, framing, roofing, windows, siding and interior finishes, all matched so the new space looks like it was always part of the house.

Room to grow

Types of additions

Every home and lot is different, so the right addition depends on how you live and how much space you have to work with. Some families need a full new wing; others just need a few extra feet to make a kitchen or bathroom work.

  • Room additions New bedrooms, family rooms, offices and in-law suites on a new foundation.
  • Bump-outs A few extra feet for a kitchen, bathroom or closet without a full addition.
  • Second-story additions Build up instead of out when the lot is tight.
  • Sunrooms and mudrooms Bright, practical spaces that connect the house to the yard.
The best way to get a bigger house is sometimes to keep the one you have.

Planning your addition

A good addition starts with a good plan. We visit, measure and talk through how you want to use the space, then help with drawings and building permits. You get a clear, written estimate and a schedule, and we keep you updated at every stage, from footings to final inspection.

  • Site visit and estimate We measure, listen and give you an honest price.
  • Plans and permits Drawings and local permits handled before work starts.
  • Build Foundation to finishes, with steady updates along the way.
  • Walk-through We finish the punch list and leave a clean site.
Clear plans, clear prices and the same crew from start to finish.
