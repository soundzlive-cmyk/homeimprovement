Roofing

New roofs and leak repairs built for New England weather, from tear-off to the last ridge cap.

Your roof takes the worst of every nor'easter, ice dam and August heat wave. When it's done right, you never have to think about it. When it isn't, small leaks turn into rotted decking, stained ceilings and wet insulation.

We handle full roof replacements, new roofs on additions and targeted repairs, with clean job sites, magnetic sweeps for nails and clear communication from the first inspection to the final walk-through.

No. 01

A new roof, from the deck up

Roof replacement

A full replacement starts with a complete tear-off down to the roof deck, so we can see and replace any soft or rotted sheathing. Then we install ice and water shield along the eaves and valleys, synthetic underlayment, new drip edge and flashing, and finish with shingles, ridge vents and ridge caps for a roof that sheds water and breathes properly.

  • Tear-off and inspection Old shingles removed and the deck checked board by board.
  • Ice and water protection Membrane at eaves, valleys and penetrations to stop ice-dam leaks.
  • Flashing and ventilation New drip edge, step flashing and ridge vents.
  • Clean-up Debris hauled away and the yard swept for nails.
No. 02

Stop the leak before it spreads

Roof repair

Not every roof needs replacing. Missing or lifted shingles, cracked pipe boots, failed flashing around chimneys and skylights, and ice-dam damage can often be repaired for a fraction of the cost. We inspect the whole roof, find where the water is actually getting in, and fix the cause rather than the symptom.

The cheapest roof is the one you fix early.
  • Common repairs Shingle replacement, pipe boots, chimney and skylight flashing, valleys.
  • Storm damage Wind and fallen-limb damage, with photos for your insurance claim.
No. 03

Built for the weather here

Roofing materials

Asphalt shingles

The most popular choice in New England. Architectural shingles offer great wind ratings, long warranties and a wide range of colors at a fair price.

Metal roofing

Standing-seam metal lasts for decades, sheds snow quickly and works well on porches, additions and low-slope sections.

Flashing & gutters

Chimneys, skylights, walls and valleys need proper flashing, and gutters carry the water away from your foundation.

We'll walk you through materials, colors and warranties, and give you a clear, written estimate.
